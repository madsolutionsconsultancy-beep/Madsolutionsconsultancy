Inbox

Inbox management

Sorting emails, deleting spam, and drafting replies so your inbox stays clean and under control. I can also set up folders and filters, flag anything urgent, and give you a daily summary so nothing important slips through.

Calendar

Calendar organization

Scheduling meetings, setting reminders, and protecting focus time to keep your day running smoothly. I'll handle rescheduling, chase confirmations, and build in buffer time so your week never feels overbooked.

Travel

Travel planning

Booking flights, hotels, and coordinating itineraries for stress-free business or personal travel. I'll compare options against your budget, put together a clear day-by-day itinerary, and stay on hand if plans change last minute.

Data entry

Updating CRM records, organizing digital files, and handling basic reports with accuracy and efficiency. From spreadsheets to cloud folders, I'll keep everything named, sorted, and easy for you to find.

Customer support

Answering support tickets, live chats, and routine customer inquiries with professional and friendly care. I follow your tone of voice so customers get a consistent, on-brand experience every time.
