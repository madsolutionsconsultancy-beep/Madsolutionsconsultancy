A little about me

Three decades in the making.

I'm 50, and this business is the sum of a career that's taken more than one shape. I studied law, psychology, and media, then spent years at Kraft Foods across customer service, maintenance, and sales — both inbound and outbound, B2B included. From there I moved into a role supporting young people, and the rest, as they say, is history.

What ties it all together is three decades of admin and organisational skills, built up job by job, alongside a lot of other skills picked up along the way. It's why I can jump into almost anyone's inbox, calendar, or to-do list and just get on with it.

Outside of work, I set up a YouTube channel in 2024 with my sister and my partner — something we love, and it keeps growing a little more each day.
